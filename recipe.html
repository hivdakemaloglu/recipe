Homemade Strawberry Cake

A soft, fluffy and delicious cake topped with fresh strawberries. The perfect dessert for a lovely afternoon!

⏰

Preparation

20 Minutes

🍰

Cooking Time

35 Minutes

👩‍🍳

Difficulty

Easy

🍓

Servings

6 People

Ingredients 🍓

Everything you need for this recipe:

  • 2 cups of flour
  • 1 cup of sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup of milk
  • Half a cup of vegetable oil
  • 1 packet of baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon of vanilla
  • Fresh strawberries

How to Make It ♡

Follow these simple steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix eggs and sugar in a bowl.
  3. Add milk and vegetable oil.
  4. Add flour, baking powder and vanilla.
  5. Mix until the batter becomes smooth.
  6. Pour the mixture into a cake pan.
  7. Bake for approximately 35 minutes.
  8. Decorate with fresh strawberries and serve.

♡ Little Kitchen Tip

Add some powdered sugar on top and decorate your cake with fresh strawberries for an extra beautiful finish!
